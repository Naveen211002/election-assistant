🇮🇳 The Indian Election Journey

Follow the 10 stages from delimitation to declaration of results

1
🗺️

Delimitation of Constituencies

The Delimitation Commission redraws the boundaries of parliamentary and assembly constituencies based on the latest Census data. This ensures equal representation — each constituency has roughly the same population.

AuthorityDelimitation Commission of India
BasisCensus population data
Last Done2008 (based on 2001 Census)
2
📝

Preparation of Electoral Rolls

The ECI continuously updates the voter list for every constituency. Citizens can register using Form 6, and existing entries can be corrected via Form 8. Special revision drives are held before major elections.

RegistrationForm 6 (new) / Form 6A (NRI)
EligibilityIndian citizen, 18+ years
PlatformVoter Helpline App / voters.eci.gov.in
3
📢

Announcement of Election Schedule

The ECI holds a press conference to announce the election schedule, including dates for nominations, polling, and counting. The Model Code of Conduct (MCC) comes into effect immediately upon announcement.

MCC EffectImmediate upon announcement
PhasesMulti-phase for large elections
Duration~6-8 weeks for general elections
4
📜

Issue of Notification

The President (for Lok Sabha) or Governor (for State Assembly) issues a formal notification calling upon constituencies to elect their representatives. This is the legal starting gun for the election process.

Lok SabhaPresident of India
Vidhan SabhaState Governor
Published inGazette of India
5
📄

Filing of Nominations

Candidates file their nomination papers with the Returning Officer. They must declare criminal cases, assets, liabilities, and educational qualifications. A security deposit is also required.

Deposit (General)₹25,000 (Lok Sabha)
Deposit (SC/ST)₹12,500 (Lok Sabha)
AffidavitCriminal record, assets, education
6
🔍

Scrutiny of Nominations

The Returning Officer examines all nomination papers for validity. Nominations can be rejected for reasons like incorrect forms, missing signatures, or failure to meet eligibility criteria (age, citizenship, etc.).

TimelineDay after last date of nomination
AuthorityReturning Officer
AppealHigh Court (Election Petition)
7
🚪

Withdrawal of Candidature

Candidates who wish to exit the race can withdraw their nomination before the deadline. After this date, the final list of contesting candidates is published by the Returning Officer.

Deadline2 days after scrutiny
After withdrawalFinal candidate list published
SymbolsAllotted after final list
8
📣

Election Campaigning

Candidates and parties campaign through rallies, door-to-door visits, social media, and advertisements. All campaigning must follow the Model Code of Conduct. Campaign stops 48 hours before polling.

Silence Period48 hours before polling
Spending Limit₹95 lakh (Lok Sabha)
OversightECI Media Certification Committee
9
🗳️

Polling Day (Voting)

Citizens cast their votes at designated polling stations using EVMs (Electronic Voting Machines) with VVPAT verification. Voters get indelible ink on their left index finger after voting.

Hours7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (typical)
ID RequiredEPIC or 12 approved photo IDs
InkIndelible ink on left index finger
10
🏆

Counting & Declaration of Results

EVMs are opened in counting halls under the Returning Officer's supervision. VVPAT slips from 5 random booths per constituency are verified. The candidate with the most votes is declared the winner.

VVPAT Check5 random booths per constituency
WinnerFirst Past The Post (FPTP)
CertificateCertificate of Election issued

📋 Voter Registration Guide

Everything you need to become a registered voter in India

✅

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen
  • 18 years or older (as of Jan 1 of revision year)
  • Ordinary resident of the constituency
  • Not disqualified under any law
  • Of sound mind and not declared so by court
📄

Documents Required

Age Proof (any one)

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Birth Certificate
  • Class X / XII Marksheet
  • Driving License

Address Proof (any one)

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Utility Bill (electricity, water, gas)
  • Rent Agreement
  • Passport
👣

Registration Steps

1
Online Method: Visit voters.eci.gov.in or download the Voter Helpline App
2
Click "New Voter Registration" and fill Form 6
3
Upload passport-size photo, age proof, and address proof
4
Submit and note your Reference Number
5
A Booth Level Officer (BLO) will visit your address for verification
6
Upon approval, your name is added to the Electoral Roll and EPIC (Voter ID) is issued
📝

Important Forms

Form 6 New voter registration
Form 6A NRI voter registration
Form 7 Objection to inclusion / request deletion
Form 8 Correction of entries in roll
Form 8A Transposition within constituency
🗳️

On Voting Day

  • Carry your EPIC (Voter ID) or any of the 12 approved photo IDs
  • Go to your assigned polling station (check on Voter Helpline App)
  • Stand in the queue; your identity will be verified
  • Indelible ink is applied on your left index finger
  • Enter the voting booth, press the button next to your chosen candidate on the EVM
  • Verify your vote on the VVPAT paper slip (visible for 7 seconds)
  • Exit the polling station — your vote is cast! 🎉
